BroncoCircle — CPP Community & Friend-Finding App
Concept
A Cal Poly Pomona–specific social app that helps students make friends and build a campus community. Its main feature, Find My Circle, connects students through shared interests and overlapping free time so they can actually meet between classes.

BroncoCircle is a working name. The project is being planned for Minihacks 2026, with roughly 8 hours of coding.
Core Features — Hackathon Priorities

1. Profiles: Name, major, interests, availability on campus, and what the student wants—lunch friends, study buddies, gym partners, or general friendships.
2. Find My Circle: Suggest small groups of 4–6 students based on interests and shared availability. Explain why each group matches.
3. Accept & Join: Students choose whether to join a suggested circle and can view its members.
4. Group Chat: Working messaging within each circle.
5. Meetup Planning: Suggest a CPP meeting spot and let members vote on a time.
   Main demo flow: Create profile → find a circle → accept → chat → agree on a meetup.

Example: Students who like soccer and share a Thursday lunch break get matched, start chatting, and plan lunch at the BSC.
Broader Social Features — If Time Allows

- Campus feed: Posts, questions, lunch invitations, and pickup games.
- Communities: Large ongoing chats for sports, gaming, commuters, and other interests.
- Class chats: Connections based on actual courses or sections.
- Between Classes: Find students who are free now and open to hanging out.
- Come With Me: Find someone to attend a campus event with.
- Individual matching: Find one friend instead of a group.
- Join matching with an existing friend; circles can welcome new members.
  These support the broader vision, but the first build should prioritize the complete circle-to-meetup experience.
  AI’s Role
  Potentially use the AI provided by the hackathon to interpret written interests, explain matches, and suggest icebreakers or meetups. Code should check actual schedule overlap. The provided AI tools and exact sponsor requirements still need verification.
  Design
  Mockups were generated with CPP forest green, gold, and cream, rounded cards, student profiles, and a friendly mobile interface. They show the campus feed, circle matching, communities, and chat with meetup polls.
  Key Product Decisions
- Make the experience feel specific to CPP through campus locations, class connections, events, and shared breaks.
- Keep matching opt-in and let students control what availability they reveal.
- Include leave, block, and report controls.
- Prioritize a polished, functioning demo across two devices.
- Tech stack and implementation are not yet finalized.
